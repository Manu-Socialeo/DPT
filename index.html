About Dogs Protection Trust

Dogs Protection Trust is a grassroots dog rescue shelter located in Mysore, Karnataka, run by a single dedicated woman with an unwavering commitment to animal welfare.

Our focus is on injured, abandoned, disabled, and senior street dogs who have nowhere else to turn. We provide them with medical care, nutritious food, and a safe haven where they can heal and live with dignity.

We operate purely on donations and volunteer support from compassionate individuals who believe every dog deserves a chance at a better life. Together, we're making a difference, one rescued dog at a time.
